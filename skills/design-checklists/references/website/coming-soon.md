# Coming Soon (Website pages)

A placeholder page shown before a product or feature launches, designed to capture interest and build an early audience.

- **What is coming** — A clear description of what is being built, enough to generate genuine interest without overpromising
  - Tip: The most effective coming soon pages give a product direction, specific enough to be compelling, vague enough not to overpromise
- **Email capture** — A simple signup form to collect email addresses from interested visitors before launch
  - Tip: A tangible incentive for signing up (early access, a discount, or exclusive updates) consistently outperforms a bare email field in conversion
- **Launch date or timeline** — An indication of when the product will be available, even if it is approximate
  - Tip: When the exact date is uncertain, a quarter or season is still more useful than no timeline at all, since even approximate dates create anticipation
- **Social links** — Links to social profiles where visitors can follow progress and updates
- **Brand presence** — A page that is visually on brand even before the product exists
