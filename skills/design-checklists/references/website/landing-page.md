# Landing Page (Website pages)

A standalone page designed to convert a specific audience, typically reached via an ad, email, or campaign link.

- **Headline** — A single, clear statement of what the product does or the value it delivers
  - Tip: The most effective headlines are written for a skeptic, assuming the visitor knows nothing and has three seconds to decide
- **Subheadline** — A supporting line that adds context or specificity to the headline claim
- **Hero visual** — An image, illustration, or product screenshot that reinforces the headline and makes the page feel tangible
  - Tip: A product screenshot or demo video consistently outperforms abstract illustration for conversion-focused landing pages
- **Primary CTA** — One clear action for the visitor to take (sign up, start a trial, book a demo)
  - Tip: Multiple CTAs on a single landing page split attention and consistently lower conversion, since one primary action is the norm on high-performing pages
- **Social proof** — Customer logos, testimonials, review scores, or user counts that validate the claim.
  - Tip: Social proof placed immediately below the fold catches skeptical visitors at exactly the point they start questioning the headline claim.
- **Key benefits** — A concise breakdown of the main reasons a visitor should care, focused on outcomes not features.
- **Objection handling** — Content that addresses the most common reasons a visitor would not convert e.g. price, complexity, commitment
  - Tip: FAQs on a landing page are the most effective format to tackling these areas
- **Repeated CTA** — The primary CTA repeated at the bottom of the page for visitors who scroll all the way through
