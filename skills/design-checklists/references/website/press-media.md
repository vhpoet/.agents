# Press / Media (Website pages)

A page providing journalists, analysts, and content creators with the resources they need to cover the company accurately.

- **Company overview** — A concise, factual description of the company, what it does, when it was founded, and who it serves
  - Tip: A press overview written as a ready-to-use paragraph saves journalists time. The easier it is to lift and paste, the more often it gets used
- **Press coverage** — A curated list of notable media mentions with publication name, headline, and link
  - Tip: Logos of well-known publications displayed visually signal credibility faster than a text list
- **Press contact** — A dedicated email address for media enquiries so journalists know exactly where to reach the right person or team
- **Brand assets** — Downloadable logos, product screenshots, and founder headshots in multiple formats
  - Tip: Light and dark logo versions are both routinely needed by journalists, since a press kit without both creates unnecessary back-and-forth
- **Key statistics** — Notable numbers that journalists can use to characterise the company e.g. customers, revenue milestones, countries, team size
- **Recent press releases** — Archived official announcements for journalists researching the company's history
