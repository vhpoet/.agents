# Affiliate (Website pages)

A page that invites potential affiliates or partners to join a programme, explaining how it works and what they stand to earn.

- **Programme overview** — A clear explanation of what the affiliate or partner programme involves and who it is designed for
- **Commission or reward structure** — Exactly what affiliates earn (percentage, flat fee, recurring commission) stated plainly
  - Tip: Ambiguous commission structures are the most common reason potential affiliates leave this page without applying
- **How it works** — A simple step-by-step breakdown of joining, sharing, and earning
  - Tip: If using a third party to connect, it's helpful to include screenshots for steps
- **Resources provided** — What materials the company provides (links, banners, copy, tracking) to help affiliates succeed
- **Social proof** — Testimonials or earnings examples from existing affiliates who are benefiting from the programme
- **Application or signup CTA** — A clear form or button to join the programme
