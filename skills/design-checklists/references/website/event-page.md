# Event Page (Website pages)

A page promoting a specific event — a conference, webinar, workshop, or meetup — and driving registrations.

- **Event name and date** — The name of the event and when it is happening, shown prominently at the top of the page
  - Tip: Include the timezone explicitly for online events
- **Event description** — What the event is, who it is for, and what attendees will get from it
- **Speakers, presenters or hosts** — Names, photos, and brief bios of the people presenting or facilitating
  - Tip: Speaker credibility is often the deciding factor for whether someone registers. It tends to be the most-read section of an event page
- **Agenda or schedule** — A session-by-session or time-slot breakdown of what will happen during the event
- **Registration CTA** — A clear form or button to register, with pricing if applicable
  - Tip: Flagging ticket availability close to CTA helps communicate scarcity
- **Location or platform details** — For in-person events, the venue address and travel information. For online events, the platform being used
